Keynotes/Invited Speakers

SEC Keynote

Converging Disruptions, Compounding Risks: How Research becomes a Critical Capability for Organisations, Industry and Societal Resilience

Dr Gayan Benedict, MIT CISR Industry Research Fellow, Partner PwC

Our society and industries are experiencing the accelerating convergence of disruptive innovation — quantum, AI, robotics, biomedical, blockchain and more — where progress in each is compounding the others, and where the industries built to serve us for over a century are unlikely to emerge looking anything like they entered. This talk draws on lessons from financial regulation, frontier technology adoption and ecosystem-scale risk to argue that research is no longer a specialist activity but transforming into a core capability for resilient organisations, regulators and the societies they serve.

Dr Benedict is a Industry Research Fellow at the MIT Sloan Center for Information Systems research and a partner in Advisory at PwC Australia. Gayan chairs the Standards Australia IT-041 Committee for Blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technologies and is the former CIO of the Reserve Bank of Australia where his accountabilities included the bank’s cyber and operational resilience and the establishment and operation of the Bank’s Innovation Lab. In 2023 Gayan was awarded a post-doctoral industry Fulbright Scholarship to the intersectional Cyber-smart Research Center at Georgetown University where he researched the risks and regulatory responses to AI-enabled blockchain systems in the financial sector.

WISE Keynote and 2026 Kristian Beckman Award Reciepient

Information Security Research: A 35-year journey

Professor Rossouw von Solms

In this presentation, Rossouw will provide a retrospective view of research in this very dynamic field of study. He will highlight how the field has evolved over this period, how his research foci had to adapt to stay aligned, how research approaches and methodologies changed and what influence these changes had on researchers. Obviously, by looking back might provide modern-day researchers in this field of study some guidelines on how position and plan future research endeavours.

Rossouw has had an illustrious career in academia with a lifetime dedicated to information security teaching and research. Over his 40+ years of service, he has predominantly concentrated on research, education and practice in the areas of; computer security (at first), information security, cyber security (in more recent years) and security management that branched into security governance.

His scholarly contributions have included more than 250 research papers with significant citations and an impressive h-index. His ranking as the global #1 Highly Ranked Scholar by ScholarGPS in both Information Security and Security Management demonstrate his standing and impact as a researcher.

His involvement and contribution to societal and organisational structures in the field of information security are commendable. His commitment, dedication, and involvement, specifically within IFIP TC11, contributed to his receiving the highly celebrated IFIP Fellow Award, acknowledging his contribution to this field.

Prof. von Solms will be presenting a keynote talk at the conference and will formally receive his award as part of the event.
